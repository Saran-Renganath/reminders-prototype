Summary
This was the third session with the client, a 34-year-old software engineer presenting with work-related anxiety and poor sleep. The client reported sleeping approximately 5 hours per night and waking at 3am with thoughts about deadlines. The session focused on continuing CBT work by identifying and examining the automatic thought 'if I miss this release I will be fired.' The client rated anxiety at 7/10 at the start of the session and 5/10 at the end. Box breathing was practiced during the session. The client mentioned their family doctor requested a short progress letter and asked about billing sessions to extended health insurance.
